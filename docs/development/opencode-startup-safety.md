# OpenCode startup without inference: reviewed limits — 2026-11-03

The live Desktop manifest selector is implemented. Strict inference-free native
startup remains **unestablished** for the reviewed OpenCode 2.2.5 executable.
No real OpenCode command, login, provider request, native database/ledger or
quarantine was accessed during this work. A startup timeout, a blank TUI, absence
of submitted input and lack of visible activity do not prove absence of inference
or billing. The current 30-minute owned-TUI deadline is not a spending cap.

## Concrete unresolved requirement

Keep the review manifest's `nativeStartupAuthorized: false` for an acceptance that
requires zero provider inference. Do not replace technical enforcement with consent
to unknown activity. The missing deliverable is a version-specific, fail-closed
startup/egress boundary covering **both TUI and private server, their extension
code and descendants**, with synthetic bypass-negative evidence and separately
reviewed native compatibility. Its allowed loopback/IPC and native-identity access
must be explicit. The existing factory does not implement this boundary.

The independent shared-write/migration and descendant-exit gates also remain. A
future native attempt needs its own bounded, exact authorization after its required
technical gates are satisfied. This increment neither requests that authorization
nor creates an executable manifest. See [the current factory/effects](opencode-desktop-terminal.md)
and [passive standalone review](opencode-standalone-review.md).
